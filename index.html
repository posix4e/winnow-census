Artifacts and provenance

    What this can establish

    A successful handshake with NODE_COMPACT_FILTERS advertises support; it does not prove that the endpoint returns valid filters. The wallet checks selected peers and filter responses during normal operation. Near-tip candidates report a nonnegative height within 100 blocks, in either direction, of the reference tip supplied by the input snapshot. Reported height alone cannot identify a chain or establish dishonesty.

    These are endpoints, not a count of distinct machines or people. One node can have several addresses, ports and transports. Multiple nodes can also share a public address. The clearnet category combines IPv4 and IPv6; Tor and I2P are measured separately. CJDNS and invalid or private addresses are excluded. Timeouts describe this observer’s connection attempt, not permanent unreachability.

    The downloadable catalog canonicalizes and deduplicates endpoints, applies public-address and network-prefix diversity rules to clearnet, validates overlay addresses, and retains at most 2,000 candidates per overlay. The wallet supports direct clearnet and opt-in Tor; I2P transport is deferred. A diagnostic sample or unhealthy overlay run cannot replace production data. Refresh failures leave the previous good artifacts available, with their original observation date.

    Probe duration is elapsed time for successful filter handshakes, including proxy connection setup where used and the bounded wait for a fee-filter announcement. It is not a round-trip latency measurement. Fee filters are the peer’s announced relay threshold, not a fee recommendation or a transaction-confirmation estimate. The split-height count records heights 961,632–961,652; it does not independently prove which chain a peer follows.

    See the census source and reproducible comparisons, full run logs and retained raw observations, and Winnow wallet. BTCNodes is our input source, so agreement with its endpoint list is not independent confirmation.
